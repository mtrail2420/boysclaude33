Overall Score — v4 (PFR-verified)
SCORE = AWARD PTS + ALL-PRO PTS + ROLE PTS
Built from real Pro Football Reference data — exact award years and exact All-Pro selection counts, not text-parsed from a write-up. Pro Bowl selections are noted in a player's record but deliberately not scored — Pro Bowl voting is too inconsistent to weight fairly. 270 of 326 players are scored this way; the rest still use the older text-based method until matched.
Every prior version scored a description of a player's career. This version scores actual records: real MVP/OPOY/DPOY/OROY/DROY/Super Bowl MVP winners by year (1957–2025) and every AP First-Team All-Pro selection by year (2007–2025), sourced from Pro Football Reference exports. Career approximate value (2016+ draft classes) drives the Role component. A player's score reflects the exact number of times they won something, not a "single vs. multiple" guess from a sentence.

Role Points
For 2016+ draftees, based on real career Approximate Value (AV) from PFR — not language. For pre-2016 draftees (not in the draft-class data), falls back to the write-up's stated role.
Career AV ≥ 18 (productive starter)+5
Career AV 5–17 (role/depth player)+2
Career AV < 5+0
Tiers

Known Limitation
Two real gaps remain, both visible on the Verify page: (1) the AP All-Pro roster data only goes back to 2007 and the draft-class AV data only covers 2016+ draftees, so pre-2016 draftees without an All-Pro or major award still lean on the older, less reliable text-based role guess. (2) 56 of 326 scored players didn't match any of the three PFR datasets by name — some genuinely have no major accolades, others may need a name-matching fix (nicknames, suffixes). Both are being worked through, tracked openly on the Verify page rather than silently assumed correct.
Wildcard Cooked Meter
A 0–100 scale graded after the fact on late/speculative picks. Lower is better: 0–15 Elite hit, 16–35 Solid value / Strong hit, 36–50 Mixed result, 51–65 Underwhelming, 66+ Cooked.
Dynasty Power Index
Site-only composite metric (not part of the original workbook): average score plus a tier-weighted bonus per pick — Legend +15, Franchise +8, HES +3, Starter 0, Contributor −3, Bust −8 — averaged across the roster. Rewards stacking difference-makers over just accumulating picks.
